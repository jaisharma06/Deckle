Privacy Policy

Deckle by Avyaan Studios · Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

Deckle does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet access. It does not request Android's network permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Camera images. Photos you take in Deckle are used only to create your scans. They are processed and stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Imported images. Pictures you choose from your gallery are copied into the app's private storage. Deckle only sees the images you pick.
  • Recognised text. Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your device. The text is stored with your documents so you can search and copy it.
  • Settings. Your preferences are stored on your device.

None of this leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself, for example through Android's share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you share it with.

Permissions

What Deckle does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising ID.
  • No cloud sync or backups to our servers. The app talks to no server at all.

Deleting your data

Delete documents in the app; they go to the trash first and can be removed permanently from there. Uninstalling Deckle, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Deckle does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.

Feedback page (website only)

The feedback page is separate from the app. If you send feedback there, what you type (your message, the topic, and, only if you fill them in, a rating, app version, phone model and email address) is stored in a Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database so we can read it. Your email is used only to reply to you. Feedback is not public, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising. To have your feedback deleted, open an issue at the address below and tell us roughly when you sent it.

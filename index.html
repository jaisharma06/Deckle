Deckle

Scan offline. Keep it yours.

Deckle turns paper into clean, searchable PDFs using only your phone. It has no account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking. The app doesn't even have internet access, so your documents stay on your device until you choose to share them.

Finds the page

Live edge detection and auto-capture, even in dim light.

Clean scans

18 filters, with shadows and uneven light removed.

Searchable text

On-device OCR. Exported PDFs are searchable.

Organised

Folders, sub-folders, search and five library views.

Share your way

PDF, JPEG, PNG, password-protected PDFs and folder ZIPs.

Private by design

Works fully offline. Nothing is collected, ever.
